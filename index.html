What is DNA fingerprinting?

Imagine your DNA is an enormous instruction book. Certain sections contain patterns that vary between people. Scientists examine selected variable regions and build a DNA profile, which can be compared with another profile.

Zoom in

Click a base

A pairs with T. G pairs with C.

DNA is the biological material (short for Deoxyribonucleic Acid). A DNA profile is a set of measurements from selected genetic markers, not a person's whole genetic code. Identical twins generally share the same inherited profile at the standard forensic markers.
